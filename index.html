🎸 90 Day Guitar Curriculum

Rock / Blues Rhythm • CAGED • Triads • Lead • Songwriting

Daily Practice

☐ Technique — 10 min
☐ Rhythm & groove — 20 min
☐ Chords / harmony — 20 min
☐ Fretboard training — 15 min
☐ Lead guitar — 15 min
☐ Ear training — 10 min
☐ Creativity / songwriting — 10 min

Month 1 — Foundation

Weeks 1–2

Open chords, rhythm timing, string 5 & 6 note memorization, pentatonic box 1.

Weeks 3–4

CAGED shapes, roots/3rds/5ths, beginning triads.

Month 2 — Harmony & Lead

Weeks 5–6

Diatonic triads, inversions, dominant 7 chords, blues progression.

Weeks 7–8

CAGED + pentatonic connection, chord-tone fills.

Month 3 — Musician Skills

Weeks 9–10

Write progressions and riffs. Create musical ideas daily.

Weeks 11–12

Melodies, arrangement, complete original song.
